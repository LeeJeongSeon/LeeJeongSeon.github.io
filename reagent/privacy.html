English

Reagent: Bloom ("the app"; sold on Steam as Reagent) is a single-player puzzle game about quietly restoring a world. It has no accounts, no ads, and plays fully offline. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what the app does — and does not — do with your data.

Data collected by us

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics. We (the developer) do not collect, store, or share any personal information on our own servers — we have none.

Purchases

The app offers a free trial section and a single one-time unlock of the full game. Payment is handled entirely by the store you bought it on — the Apple App Store, Google Play, or Steam — under that store's own privacy policy (Apple, Google, Valve). We never receive your name, address, or payment details. To know whether the full game is unlocked, the app asks the store whether your store account owns the unlock and keeps a simple yes/no record on your device so the game stays unlocked even offline. The "Restore Purchases" button simply asks the store again.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your game progress locally on your device only: restored regions, the flora you have collected in the codex, your current position in the world, settings (such as sound), and the unlock record described above. This local data is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is removed when you delete the app.

Third-party services

None. The app contains no advertising networks, no analytics SDKs, and no tracking of any kind. The only outside parties involved are the store platforms themselves, for purchases as described above.

Platform statistics

Apple, Google, and Valve may show us aggregate, anonymized statistics (such as crash reports or download counts) that they collect under their own policies and your device's own privacy settings. These contain no information that identifies you, and we receive nothing else.

Children

The app is a general-audience game. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children, and shows no ads.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised.

Contact

Questions? Email contact@naraelab.com.
